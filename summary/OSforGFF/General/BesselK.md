## Overview

The modified Bessel function of the second kind of arbitrary order and the single Schwinger identity
behind every dimension's proper-time evaluation. Defines

$$K_\nu(z) = \int_0^\infty e^{-z \cosh t}\,\cosh(\nu t)\,dt$$

(`besselK`) and proves the **master Schwinger identity**

$$\int_0^\infty t^{\nu-1}\,e^{-m^2 t - r^2/(4t)}\,dt = 2\,(r/2m)^\nu\,K_\nu(mr)$$

(`schwingerIntegral_eq_besselK`), by the change of variables $t = (r/2m)e^u$ followed by a
$\nu$-generic symmetrization of the resulting full-line integral (with cleaner Gaussian-domination
integrability than the per-order proofs it replaces).

At spacetime dimension $d$ the proper-time integrand carries $t^{-d/2}$, i.e. $\nu = 1 - d/2$, so
every instance is one order of this identity, using evenness $K_{-\nu} = K_\nu$ (`besselK_neg`).
