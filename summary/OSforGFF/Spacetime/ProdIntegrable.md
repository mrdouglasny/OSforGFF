## Overview

Establishes integrability and order-$N$ boundary-vanishing decay bounds for Schwartz test
functions that vanish on the nonpositive-time half-space, as needed for the OS3 reflection
positivity argument, over a generic dimension `{d : ℕ}` with `[Fact (2 ≤ d)]`.
The key steps are: (1) the vanishing of the time derivative on the half-space; (2) the
order-$N$ FTC + Schwartz-decay bound
$\lVert f(t, x_{\mathrm{sp}})\rVert \leq C t^N / (1 + \lVert x_{\mathrm{sp}}\rVert)^d$
(by induction on $N$); and (3) the order-$N$ bound $G(t) \leq Ct^N$ for the spatial norm
integral $G(t) = \int_{\mathbb{R}^{d-1}} \lVert f(t, x)\rVert\,dx$ (used at $N = d$ by the
OS3 Fubini layer). Supporting machinery includes
the time-space construction `spacetimeOfTimeSpace` $(t, x) \mapsto (t, x_1, \ldots, x_{d-1})$
with its coordinate, decomposition, and norm lemmas, and integrability of the polynomial
decay $(1 + \lVert x\rVert)^{-d}$ on the spatial slice $\mathbb{R}^{d-1}$.

### [`schwartz_vanishing_pow_decay`](../../OSforGFF/Spacetime/ProdIntegrable.lean#L243) — Theorem

**Statement**: Order-$N$ boundary-vanishing bound with spatial decay: for a complex Schwartz function $f$ vanishing on $\{x_0 \le 0\}$ and any $N$, there is $C > 0$ with, for all $t > 0$ and $x_{\mathrm{sp}} \in \mathbb{R}^{d-1}$,
$$\lVert f(t, x_{\mathrm{sp}})\rVert \leq \frac{C \cdot t^N}{(1 + \lVert x_{\mathrm{sp}}\rVert)^d}.$$
The case $N = 1$ is the fundamental-theorem estimate; the general case follows by induction, applying the inductive bound to the time derivative (again Schwartz and boundary-vanishing) and integrating along the time direction.
