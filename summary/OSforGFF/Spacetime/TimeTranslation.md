## Overview

Builds the time-translation one-parameter group $T_s$ at three levels — spacetime
points, Schwartz test functions (real and complex), and tempered distributions
(field configurations) — the machinery underlying the OS4 (Ergodicity) axiom.
Everything is dimension-generic in the `TimeTranslation` namespace over the
section variables `{d : ℕ} [Fact (2 ≤ d)]`; there are no `…4` shorthands in this
file (many lemmas even `omit [Fact (2 ≤ d)]`, holding for all `d`). On spacetime,
`timeShift s` adds $s$ to the time coordinate $x_0$ and fixes the spatial
coordinates; it is an affine isometry (hence antilipschitz and measure-preserving)
with temperate growth, and it forms a group in $s$. Composition with `timeShift s`
gives the continuous linear operators `timeTranslationSchwartz` /
`timeTranslationSchwartzℂ` on Schwartz space, and the dual composition gives
`timeTranslationDistribution` on field configurations. The analytic core is a
locally uniform Lipschitz seminorm bound `schwartz_timeTranslation_lipschitz_seminorm`
(proved via the fundamental theorem of calculus / mean value theorem, the chain
rule for iterated derivatives, and a Peetre weight inequality), from which
`continuous_timeTranslationSchwartz` — continuity of $s \mapsto T_s f$ on Schwartz
space — follows by reduction to continuity at $s = 0$.

### [`schwartz_timeTranslation_lipschitz_seminorm`](../../../OSforGFF/Spacetime/TimeTranslation.lean#L379) — Theorem

**Statement**: Locally uniform Lipschitz bound for time translation on the Schwartz seminorms:
$$\lVert T_h f - f\rVert_{k,n} \le \lvert h\rvert\,(1 + \lvert h\rvert)^k\,2^k\,\bigl(\lVert f\rVert_{k,n+1} + \lVert f\rVert_{0,n+1} + 1\bigr).$$
The $2^k$ factor comes from Peetre's inequality; for $\lvert h\rvert \le 1$ the prefactor is at most $4^k$, which suffices for continuity at $h = 0$.

**Proof uses**: `SchwartzMap.seminorm_le_bound`, [`iteratedFDeriv_timeTranslationSchwartz`](../../../OSforGFF/Spacetime/TimeTranslation.lean#L338), the mean value theorem, [`peetre_weight_bound`](../../../OSforGFF/Spacetime/TimeTranslation.lean#L322), `continuousMultilinearCurryLeftEquiv`
