## Overview

This file is the dimension-generic Parseval bridge: everything the OS proofs need about the covariance pairing of Schwartz test functions, derived once for every dimension $d$ from the two `GFFPropagator` fields. The central identity is the Parseval bridge for the quadratic pairing,
$$\mathrm{Re}\,\langle f, C\bar f\rangle = \int_{\mathbb{R}^d} \lVert \hat f(k)\rVert^2\,P(k)\,dk, \qquad P(k) = \frac{1}{(2\pi)^2\lVert k\rVert^2 + m^2},$$
from which positivity of the covariance is immediate, since the momentum weight $P$ is pointwise positive. The derivation never touches the closed-form kernel: since $C(x,y) = \mathrm{Cprofile}\,\lVert x-y\rVert$ agrees with the proper-time covariance $C_S$ away from the (null) diagonal, the pairing is computed against $C_S$ throughout, in four steps — (1) a shear/Fubini change of variables collapses the double integral to $\int C_S(\lVert z\rVert)\,A(z)\,dz$ with $A(z) = \int f(x)\,\overline{f(x-z)}\,dx$ the autocorrelation; (2) evenness of the kernel and $A(-z) = \overline{A(z)}$ replace $A$ by $\overline{A}$; (3) Fourier inversion identifies $\overline{A}$ as the forward Fourier transform of the momentum density $B(k) = \hat f(k)\,\overline{\hat f(k)}$; (4) the multiplication formula $\int C_S \cdot \mathcal{F}B = \int \mathcal{F}C_S \cdot B$ together with $\mathcal{F}C_S = P$ gives the momentum-space form.

Around this core the file provides: regularity of the proper-time kernel (measurability, integrability of the $t$-slices, continuity on $(0,\infty)$ — the analytic inputs for OS1 and OS4); the bilinear and sesquilinear pairings `freeCovarianceℂ_bilinear` and `freeCovarianceℂ` with product-space integrability; time-reflection and Euclidean invariance of the kernel and the double integrals; the full bilinear algebra of the pairing; the square-root momentum weight $1/\sqrt{(2\pi)^2\lVert k\rVert^2 + m^2}$ with the $L^\infty$-multiplier machinery on $L^2$ used by the square-root propagator embedding; and the centered kernel $K(z) = C(0,z)$ with continuity off the origin, integrability, and exponential/polynomial decay beyond unit radius — the OS4 inputs.
