## Overview

Defines the real covariance bilinear form
$$C_m(f, g) = \int\!\!\int f(x)\, C(x,y)\, g(y)\, dx\, dy$$
on real Schwartz test functions (with $C$ the position-space kernel `freeCovariance d m`), and
realizes it as a *squared Hilbert-space norm*. The central construction is the square-root
propagator embedding: composing the Fourier transform with multiplication by
$\sqrt{P_d}(k) = 1/\sqrt{(2\pi)^2\lVert k\rVert^2 + m^2}$ (`freePropagatorMomSqrt`) gives an
$\mathbb{R}$-linear map $T : \mathscr{S}(\mathbb{R}^d) \to L^2(\mathbb{R}^d; \mathbb{C})$ with
$$C_m(f, f) = \lVert T f\rVert_{L^2}^2,$$
by the Parseval bridge together with $\sqrt{P_d}\cdot\sqrt{P_d} = P_d$, where
$P_d(k) = 1/((2\pi)^2\lVert k\rVert^2 + m^2)$ is the momentum-space propagator.

Everything is dimension-generic: the section variables are `{d : ℕ} [Fact (2 ≤ d)]` and the free
propagator enters only through the `[GFFPropagator d m]` typeclass. This factorization carries the
analytic content of the Minlos construction — it yields **positivity** and **continuity** of the
quadratic form $f \mapsto C_m(f,f)$ in the Schwartz topology (the Minlos hypotheses; see
`Measure/Construct.lean`) — and the closing lemmas record the form's symmetry, bilinearity, zero
laws, and time-reflection invariance (used by OS3).
