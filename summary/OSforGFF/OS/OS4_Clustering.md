## Overview

Proves OS-4 clustering for the Gaussian Free Field: the joint generating functional
$\mathbb{E}\bigl[e^{\langle\omega,f\rangle + \langle T_s\omega,g\rangle}\bigr]$ approaches the
product $\mathbb{E}[e^{\langle\omega,f\rangle}]\cdot\mathbb{E}[e^{\langle\omega,g\rangle}]$ as the
separation grows — both a qualitative form (`gaussianFreeField_satisfies_OS4`) and a quantitative
polynomial form with any decay rate $\alpha > 0$ (`gaussianFreeField_satisfies_OS4_PolynomialClustering`).
The mechanism is Gaussian factorization $Z[f + T_a g] = Z[f]\,Z[g]\,\exp(-S_2(f, T_a g))$ combined

$\lVert y\rVert = \lVert x\rVert/2$.
