## Overview

Proves OS-1 (Regularity / temperedness) for the Gaussian Free Field: the exponential
bound $\lVert Z[f]\rVert \le \exp\!\big(c\,(\int \lVert f\rVert + \int \lVert f\rVert^2)\big)$
with $p = 2$ and $c = 1/(2m^2)$, together with local integrability of the two-point function.
The exponential estimate runs:

1. $\lVert Z[f]\rVert = \exp(-\tfrac12\,\mathrm{Re}\,C_\mathbb{C}(f,f))$ (from $\lvert e^z\rvert = e^{\mathrm{Re}\,z}$);
2. bilinearity gives $\mathrm{Re}\,C_\mathbb{C}(f,f) = C(\mathrm{Re}\,f,\mathrm{Re}\,f) - C(\mathrm{Im}\,f,\mathrm{Im}\,f)$, and positivity of $C$ yields $-\mathrm{Re}\,C_\mathbb{C}(f,f) \le C(\mathrm{Im}\,f,\mathrm{Im}\,f)$;
3. in momentum space (Parseval) $C(\mathrm{Im}\,f,\mathrm{Im}\,f) = \int \lVert\widehat{\mathrm{Im}\,f}(k)\rVert^2\,P_d(k)\,dk \le \tfrac{1}{m^2}\int\lVert f\rVert^2$, using the momentum bound $P_d(k) = 1/((2\pi)^2\lVert k\rVert^2 + m^2) \le 1/m^2$ (proven inline), Plancherel, and $\lvert\mathrm{Im}\,f\rvert \le \lVert f\rVert$.
