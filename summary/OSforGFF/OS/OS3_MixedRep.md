## Overview

Derives the **mixed (momentum–position) representation** of the covariance bilinear form
$\langle \Theta f, C f\rangle$ by carrying out the Fubini exchanges justified in
`OS3_MixedRepInfra`. The derivation chain is:

1. **Schwinger → heat kernel**: $\langle \Theta f, C f\rangle = \int_0^\infty e^{-sm^2}\bigl[\int\int \overline{f(x)}\,f(y)\,H_d(s,\lVert \Theta x - y\rVert)\bigr]\,ds$.
2. **Fourier of the heat kernel** introduces spatial momenta $\bar k$.
3. **$k_0$ Gaussian integral**: $\int e^{ik_0(x_0+y_0)}e^{-sk_0^2}\,dk_0 = \sqrt{\pi/s}\,e^{-(x_0+y_0)^2/4s}$.
4. **Laplace transform in $s$**: $\int_0^\infty s^{-1/2}e^{-(x_0+y_0)^2/4s - s\omega^2}\,ds = \sqrt{\pi/\omega^2}\,e^{-\omega\lvert x_0+y_0\rvert}$.

The final Bessel $K_{1/2}$ identity is

$\bar k \in \mathbb{R}^{d-1}$.
